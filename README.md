#  ShramikCare

# Digital Health Record Management System for Migrant Workers

ShramikCare is a digital health record management system designed to help migrant workers store, manage, and access their medical information in a simple, secure, and portable way.

---

##  Problem Statement

Migrant workers frequently move from one location to another for employment. Because of this, their medical records may be lost, unavailable, or difficult to access when healthcare is needed.

## Major Challenges

- 📄 Loss or unavailability of medical records
- 🏥 Difficulty accessing previous medical history
- 🌐 Language barriers
- 📱 Limited access to smartphones and digital services
- 🔄 Repeated medical tests due to missing records
- 👨‍⚕️ Difficulty for healthcare providers to access previous health information

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**ShramikCare** provides a digital platform for maintaining essential health records of migrant workers.

The system helps workers and healthcare providers access relevant medical information quickly while reducing dependency on physical documents.

### Our solution focuses on:

- 🔐 Secure health record management
- 📱 Easy access to medical information
- 📲 QR-based health record access
- 🌐 Multilingual communication
- 🎤 Voice-based interaction
- 📄 Digital storage of medical documents
- ⚡ Quick access to health history

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Digital Health Profile
Create and manage a digital profile containing essential worker and health information.

### 🏥 Medical Record Management
Store and manage important medical records and health information digitally.

### 📱 QR-Based Access
QR technology can be used to quickly access the required health information.

### 🔍 OCR Support
OCR technology can help extract information from uploaded medical documents.

### 🎤 Voice Interaction
Voice-based features help users interact with the system more easily.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
The platform is designed to support multiple languages and reduce language barriers.

### 🔐 Secure Authentication
User authentication helps protect personal and medical information.

### 👥 Role-Based Access
Different users can be provided with appropriate access based on their role.

---
